This is the first entry in the notebook — a place for longer-form writing that doesn't fit on a CV or in a paper's results section: dispatch data that raised more questions than it answered, notes from fieldwork, and analysis of what's actually happening in Bangladesh's power sector.

## Why write this in public

Most of the interesting parts of research — the dead ends, the sanity checks, the "wait, why does the data look like *that*" moments — never make it into a paper. This is where those go.

## What to expect

- Notes on grid operation and dispatch patterns, grounded in real data rather than stylized models
- Write-ups that sit between a paper and a policy memo — for readers who want the substance without the LaTeX
- Occasional posts on the tools behind the work: optimization pipelines, simulation setups, and the data engineering that makes the analysis possible

## How this blog works

No build step, no CMS. Every post is a plain Markdown file in `blog/posts/`, listed in `blog/posts.json`, and rendered client-side. Adding a new post means adding one file and one line — see the `README.md` at the root of this repository for the exact steps.
