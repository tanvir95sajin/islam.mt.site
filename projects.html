Determination of Bus Fault Current and Breaker Ratings for LG and LLG Faults

2024

MATLAB · PSAF

  • Developed a detailed MATLAB-based power system model to simulate LG and LLG faults, analyzing fault currents across critical buses under various network conditions.
  • Determined optimal circuit breaker ratings based on simulated fault current magnitudes, ensuring alignment with industry safety standards and operational reliability.
  • Provided practical, data-driven recommendations for protective device selection, contributing to improved fault management strategies in modern power system engineering.
  • Mentored by Dr. Nasim Ahmed Dewan and Fatin Ishraq Faruqi.

Meat (Beef) Freshness Detection Using E-Nose System

2024

MATLAB · Python

  • Designed and implemented an Electronic Nose (E-Nose) system using a gas sensor array to detect volatile organic compounds (VOCs) associated with beef spoilage.
  • Applied machine learning algorithms to analyze sensor data, enabling accurate classification of beef samples into fresh, moderately aged, and spoiled categories.
  • Demonstrated a practical, low-cost solution for real-time freshness detection, contributing to improved quality control in the meat industry and broader food monitoring applications.
  • Mentored by Dr. Kamrul Hasan and Nawshad Binta Nizam Ananna.
