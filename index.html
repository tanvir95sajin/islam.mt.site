What I’m Working Toward

Graduated in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), with research interests spanning power system optimization, energy economics and the low-carbon energy transition. My work centers on the modeling, optimization and analytical evaluation of modern power systems, with particular emphasis on carbon-aware system operation, security-constrained optimization and the integration of advanced generation technologies.

I’m motivated by computational methodologies that support resilient, economically efficient and sustainable power system planning and operation. Furthermore, I keep an active interest in nuclear energy systems and other advanced low-carbon technologies as components of a future decarbonized grid.

Current Focus

Three threads I keep pulling on

Q-PACERS (DSMC)

Carbon-aware OPF

Simulation-based analysis of security-constrained, carbon-aware optimal power flow for low-carbon grid operation, including how supercritical CO₂ cycles behave inside an OPF framework.

Grid analytics

Regime identification

Hidden Markov Models for identifying operational regimes in Bangladesh’s power grid, built on a decade of hourly dispatch data rather than static clustering snapshots.

Applied writing

Policy-facing analysis

Translating dispatch and tariff data into plain analysis of what’s actually happening in the sector — for engineers, policymakers, and anyone trying to follow along.
